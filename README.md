# Ad Away

Ad Away is a fast-paced desktop management game developed in Unity where players take on the role of an employee whose only job is to close an endless stream of intrusive pop-up advertisements before time runs out.

Throughout the month of July, players must earn enough money to fulfill their dream of taking their family on a vacation to the Bahamas.

---

## Gameplay

Each workday lasts 30 seconds.

During a shift, different types of advertisements continuously appear on the computer screen. Closing advertisements rewards the player with money, while making mistakes can cost valuable earnings.

At the end of every workday, the player can purchase upgrades and consumable items from the shop to improve their performance for future shifts.

The game lasts until the end of July.

- Earn **€1,000** before July ends to win.
- Fail to reach the goal and you'll receive the bad ending.

---

## Features

- Multiple advertisement types with unique mechanics
- Dynamic difficulty progression throughout the month
- Shop system with consumable items and upgrades
- Inventory system
- Countdown timer with warning effects
- Randomized advertisement spawning
- Sound effects and background music
- Multiple ending sequences
- Retro desktop-inspired interface
- Fully playable in the browser using WebGL

---

## Advertisement Types

### Close Button Ad
A simple advertisement that can be closed by pressing the close button inside of the ad.

### Buy Ad
This ad has a buy button that make you loose money if you click it.

### Multiple Click Ad
Requires multiple clicks before closing.

### CAPTCHA Ad
Players must solve a simple CAPTCHA before the advertisement closes.

---

## Shop Items

### Antivirus
Removes 5 ads from the screen.

### Antivirus Premium
Removes 10 ads from the screen.

### Clover
Adds 1 euro to each ad that you close.

### Magnifier
Makes the close button bigger to make it easer to close the ad.

### Coffee
Adds 30 seconds to a work day.
